Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

boothie is built so that your photos never leave your phone. We don't run servers that store your photos, we don't use analytics or ads, and you don't need an account.

Your photos

  • Every photo you take or pick in boothie is processed (“developed”) entirely on your device.
  • Photos and strips are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • Your strips are kept in the app's Archive on your phone, and saved to your Photos library only when you choose to save them.
  • If you share a strip, it goes only where you send it, using the iOS share sheet.

Permissions

  • Camera — to take your photo booth shots.
  • Photos — to pick photos from your library, and to save your strips there.

boothie does not use your microphone, your location, or your contacts. You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Purchases

boothie Pro is an in-app subscription sold through Apple. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your name, email, or payment details.

To check whether you have boothie Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, basic device information (such as the iOS version and country of your App Store account), and your purchase history (such as which plan you bought and when). This is used only to unlock Pro and to restore your purchases. It is not linked to your identity and is not used for advertising or tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

You can manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No accounts, sign-ups, or email collection.

Deleting your data

Your strips live only on your phone. You can delete any strip from the Archive inside the app, and deleting boothie removes everything it stored. Strips you saved to your Photos library stay there until you delete them in Photos. To ask RevenueCat to delete the anonymous purchase record tied to your app, contact us below.

Children

boothie does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
