Vocabi Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

Vocabi does not collect your personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your words and progress stay on your iPhone. The only thing that leaves the device is what is needed to handle your subscription.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you do in Vocabi is stored only on your device:

  • The words you mark as known or new, your review schedule and your Library.
  • Your settings: level, theme and reminder times.
  • The word shown in the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. The app shares it with its widget through a storage area on your iPhone that only Vocabi can read.

We never receive any of this. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Reminders

Word reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Vocabi does not use push notifications and does not create a push token, so no server sends you messages or knows when you get them.

Pronunciation

When you tap to hear a word, it is spoken by the voices built into iOS, on your device. No audio is recorded, and Vocabi does not use the microphone.

Sharing

When you share a word, Vocabi makes the image on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send it.

Subscriptions and RevenueCat

Payments are handled by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your name, email, Apple ID or payment details.

To check whether you have an active subscription, Vocabi uses RevenueCat. When the app opens, RevenueCat creates a random, anonymous user ID for your copy of the app. It is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID. RevenueCat receives:

  • This anonymous user ID.
  • Your purchase and subscription history from the App Store, such as the plan, start date, trial and renewal status.
  • Basic technical information needed to process the request, such as the app version, iOS version, device model, country and store region.

We use this only to unlock Vocabi Premium and to restore your purchase. It is not used for advertising or sold to anyone. RevenueCat processes it under its own privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • No sign-up or account.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data shared with data brokers.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Word content

Definitions come from Wiktionary under the CC BY-SA 4.0 license. They are bundled inside the app, so looking up a word does not send anything over the network.

Children

Vocabi is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Delete the app to erase everything stored on your device. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your Apple Account settings. If you want the anonymous subscription record held by RevenueCat deleted, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
