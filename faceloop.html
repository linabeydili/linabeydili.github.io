FaceLoop Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

FaceLoop (“FaceLoop”, “we”, “us”) is an iOS app that retouches photos of your face using a personal AI model built from photos you choose. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, who else processes it, and how you can have it deleted.

The short version

  • There is no sign-up. You are identified only by a random ID created on your device. We don’t ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Only the photos you choose leave your device. We never upload your whole photo library.
  • Your photos are used only to build your personal model and retouch your photos. They are not used to train models for anyone else.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.
  • You can delete your photos in the app, or ask us to delete everything.

1. Information we collect

Random account ID

The first time you open FaceLoop, the app creates a random identifier and saves it in your device’s secure storage (the iOS Keychain). If you add more than one face, each face gets its own random ID. These IDs are how our server keeps your photos and model separate from everyone else’s. They are not linked to your name, Apple ID or contact details.

Reference photos

During setup you choose several photos of your face, from your library or by taking them with the camera. The app finds the face on your device and uploads a cropped copy to our server. There, each photo is checked automatically for quality (for example, whether exactly one face is visible, the head angle, sharpness and exposure). We store these photos and the quality results so we can build and later rebuild your personal model. You can see and delete them in the app.

Your personal model

Your usable reference photos are sent to our AI provider to train a small AI model of your face. We store the finished model file on our server and use it only to retouch photos for you.

Photos you retouch

When you retouch a photo, that photo is uploaded to our server and sent, together with a mask of your face and your personal model, to our AI provider, which generates the retouched result. We do not keep a separate copy of the photo you uploaded for retouching. We do store the retouched result so the app can show and download it; the link the app receives expires after a short time.

Face data

FaceLoop works with images of faces, so please read this part carefully.

  • On your device: the app detects where faces are in a photo so you can pick yours and crop it. This happens on your phone.
  • On our server: we detect faces to check photo quality and to place the retouch precisely. If a photo you retouch contains several people, we compare the faces in it with your reference photos to find yours, so only your face is changed. The face measurements used for this comparison are computed on the fly and are not stored.
  • We do not use face data to identify you to anyone, to recognize you in other people’s photos, or for any purpose other than providing the retouching you asked for.

Notification token

If you allow notifications, we store a push notification token for your device so we can tell you when your model is ready or a retouch has finished. The free-trial reminder is scheduled on your device and does not involve our server.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription or trial. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase and subscription history from Apple. We never see or receive your payment card details.

Device permissions

  • Photo library: used to show your photos inside the app so you can pick one. Only the photos you select are uploaded.
  • Camera: used only when you choose to take a new photo.
  • Microphone: used only if you record a video with the camera. Taking photos does not use it.
  • Notifications: optional, used as described above.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Technical information

Like any internet service, our servers and hosting provider receive basic technical information with each request, such as your IP address and the time of the request, and keep limited server logs for security and troubleshooting.

What we don’t collect

We don’t collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or advertising identifier. We don’t use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.

2. How we use information

  • To build your personal model and retouch the photos you choose.
  • To show you your reference photos and results.
  • To send the notifications you allowed.
  • To manage your subscription and free trial.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.

We do not sell or rent your information, use it for advertising, or use your photos to train AI models for anyone other than you.

4. Where data is stored

Our servers are located in the United States. If you use FaceLoop from another country, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States and wherever our providers operate.

5. How long we keep data

  • Reference photos and your model: until you delete them in the app or ask us to delete your data.
  • Retouched results: stored so they can be delivered to you, and deleted along with the rest of your data on request.
  • Photos uploaded for retouching: not stored by us after processing.
  • Push tokens: until you remove a face from the app, turn off notifications, or the token stops working.
  • Server logs: kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.

Please note: deleting the app or removing a face in the app does not by itself delete data on our server. Because there is no login, we can’t link the data back to you afterwards unless you contact us first. If you want everything deleted, please contact us before deleting the app.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete photos: you can delete any reference photo from within the app, which removes it from our server.
  • Delete everything: email us and we will delete your photos, model, results and push tokens.
  • Access or correction: you can ask what we hold about you.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights, such as the right to access, delete, or object to the processing of your information, and to complain to a data protection authority. We respond to all requests regardless of where you live. Where required, we process your information to perform our service for you (contract), and for photos of your face, based on your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time by deleting your photos or contacting us.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and stored files are only reachable through short-lived signed links. Your random ID is kept in your device’s secure storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Photos of other people

Only upload reference photos of yourself, or of someone who has given you permission. When a photo contains other people, FaceLoop retouches only the selected face and leaves other faces unchanged.

9. Children

FaceLoop is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has provided us with photos, contact us and we will delete them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the “Last updated” date. For significant changes, we will let you know in the app.
